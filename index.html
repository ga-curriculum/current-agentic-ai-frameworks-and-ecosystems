Section 02

Framework Categories

Four types serve different architectural needs. Click each card to explore. There is no universal best — the right choice depends on your project constraints.

01
⚙️

Low-Level Orchestration

LangChain · LangGraph — modular, code-first.

Tap to expand ↓

Compose agents from interchangeable components with fine-grained control over every execution step. Developer-focused — requires coding skills. Supports chains, agents, graphs, and custom patterns. Best for: Complex architectures requiring maximum control and customization. Not beginner-friendly.
02
🤝

Multi-Agent Coordination

CrewAI · AutoGen — role-based agent teams.

Tap to expand ↓

Each agent has a specific role, persona, and expertise. Agents can delegate sub-tasks and collaborate toward shared goals. Best for: Complex problems requiring diverse specialist perspectives — like a researcher + writer + reviewer team. Emerging production readiness.
03
🤖

Autonomous Experiment

AutoGPT — recursive, high-autonomy.

Tap to expand ↓

Agent creates its own sub-tasks and executes them recursively with minimal human intervention. Hard to steer, predict, or constrain. Demonstrated what autonomous agents could do — and what could go wrong. Best for: Research and proof-of-concept only. Not production-ready.
04
🎛️

No-Code / Low-Code

GPTs Builder · Flowise · Zapier AI

Tap to expand ↓

Build simple agents in minutes — drag-and-drop UI, no engineering required. Business-user friendly. Limited architecture customization — you can't modify internal agent logic deeply. Best for: Quick prototypes, simple automations, and non-technical teams. Production-ready for straightforward use cases.
